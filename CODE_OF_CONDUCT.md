# Code of Conduct

JSM is a draft project. We expect all contributors to help keep participation respectful and constructive, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualized language, intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption of technical discussion.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove or revise contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Apply expectations consistently and prioritize the safety and privacy of anyone reporting a concern.

At this foundation stage, the project has not designated a public conduct-reporting address or response-time commitment. Do not include private personal information in a public issue. A private reporting contact must be published before the project invites broad community participation.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant approach and may be revised by a reviewed project decision.
